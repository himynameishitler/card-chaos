How to play

A / D or arrows: move

W / Space / ↑: jump; press again in midair to double jump

Mouse / trackpad: aim; hold click to shoot

Esc / P: pause or resume

The loser of each round picks an upgrade card. Cards stack. First to 5 round wins takes the match.
